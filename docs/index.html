About

RoadTripper, untethered

This edition runs entirely in your browser—there is no RoadTripper server and no account. Live map, geocoder, Wikipedia, OpenStreetMap, and routing requests go directly to their public providers.

Three ways to travel

Drive follows your live location. Explore turns any map click into a narrated stop. Route connects selected waypoints and researches towns along the road.

Privacy

Trip history is stored in this browser's local storage. Clear it any time from Trips. Public providers receive coordinates only when needed to answer a place or route request.
